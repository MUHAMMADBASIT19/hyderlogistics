About Us

A logistics partner built on reliability.

Hyder Logistics is an international freight company founded and led by Aliyan Haider. We handle sea shipments from booking to port clearance, so importers and exporters can focus on their business instead of paperwork and delays.

Our team works directly with shipping lines, terminals and customs agents to keep cargo moving and to keep you informed at every stage.

  • Direct coordination with carriers and terminals
  • Documentation handled by experienced staff
  • Honest updates, including when plans change

"Every container is a customer's promise to their own client. We treat it that way."

Aliyan Haider Owner, Hyder Logistics
